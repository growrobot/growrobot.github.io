Why not robots?

Robots that handle objects they have never seen, with bodies they have never used, and get better the more they are used.

GROW Lab builds General-purpose Robots for the Open World.
We study how robots perceive, act and keep learning in the real world, from grippers and dexterous hands to humanoids.

Research questions

Why aren't robots in our homes yet?

Because the real world keeps changing: new objects, new robot bodies, and everything after deployment. We ask one question for each.

Open-World Manipulation

Can a robot handle an object it has never seen, with no CAD model?

  • Model-free 6D pose and scale
  • Novel and fast-moving objects
  • Skills from one demonstration

Related: Any6D, Event6D, GraspClutter6D

Cross-Embodiment

Can the same skill run on a new gripper, hand or camera without retraining?

  • One grasp model, many grippers
  • Markerless hand-eye calibration
  • Viewpoint-robust VLA policies

Related: XGrasp, GraspGraphNet

Lifelong Robot Learning

Can a deployed robot keep improving from its own successes and failures?

  • Test-time and domain adaptation
  • Automatic 6D labeling of collected data
  • Failure detection and recovery

Related: TTA-COPE, UDA-COPE

Taeyeop Lee

Principal Investigator · Senior Researcher, AI Robot Institute, KIST · Ph.D., KAIST

Taeyeop is a Senior Researcher at the AI Robot Institute, KIST. He received his Ph.D. and M.S. from KAIST and his B.S. from Kwangwoon University. He has been fortunate to collaborate with NVIDIA Research, Meta and Adobe Research, and is a recipient of the Presidential Award of Korea.
